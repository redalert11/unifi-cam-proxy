Enable Third-Party Compatibility in the Tapo app Click to show steps
  1. Open the Tapo app and login
  2. Tap "Me"
  3. Tap "Tapo Lab"
  4. Tap "Third-Party Compatibility"
  5. Tap "Enable"
Enable Camera Account on the camera Click to show steps
  1. Open the Tapo app and login
  2. Select the camera
  3. Open Settings
  4. Open "Advanced Settings"
  5. Tap "Camera Account"
  6. Enable "Camera Account"
  7. Tap Verify your account and set username and password
Reboot each camera after enabling Third-Party Compatibility or Camera Account
